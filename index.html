Our Story

A love rooted
in simple moments 爱，扎根于平凡的时光

Nearly a decade ago, Chester and Elbee met at church, knowing each other only from afar. Two years ago, in God's perfect timing, their paths crossed again and love began to bloom.

近十年前，Chester 与 Elbee 在教会相识，却没有太多交集。直到两年前， 在上帝最美好的时间里，他们渐渐走近彼此，爱情也悄然萌芽。

With grateful hearts, they invite you to witness the beautiful beginning of their forever.

如今，他们怀着感恩的心，诚挚邀请您一同见证幸福旅程的开始。

The beginning of forever 永远的起点

The Hometown Visit回乡之旅

The trip home to meet the parents. Familiar streets, a hillside above Penang, sunny day , lovely dear

回家见父母的旅程，熟悉的街道，槟城的山峦，晴朗的日子，还有身边心爱的人。

The Journey同行的日子

One bright day, one small island. A short trip that needed no occasion, only each other, and the quiet joy of going nowhere in particular, together.

一个晴朗的日子，一座小岛。一趟不需要理由的短途旅行，只要有彼此，漫无目的地同行，也是安静的幸福。

The Proposal求婚那天

Then, at last, the day came. On one knee, surrounded by petals and everyone they love, Chester asked the only question left to ask, and Elbee said yes.

终于，那一天来了。单膝跪地，在花瓣与至亲好友的围绕中，Chester 问出了那个唯一的问题，Elbee 说，我愿意。
